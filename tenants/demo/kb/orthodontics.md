# Braces and clear aligners

Our orthodontist treats crooked teeth and bite problems in teenagers and adults, with braces or clear aligners. Start with an orthodontic consultation: the doctor examines the bite, takes photos and X-rays and suggests options.

Clear aligners are nearly invisible and must be worn about 22 hours a day; treatment usually lasts 6-18 months. Aligner planning includes a 3D scan and a digital simulation of the result.
