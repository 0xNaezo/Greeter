# Payment

We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay) and cash in hryvnias. You pay at the reception desk after the visit.

The cost of treatment is agreed in advance: after the consultation the doctor gives you a written treatment plan with an estimate. Prices marked "from" depend on the complexity and are confirmed in the plan.

For implants, crowns and clear aligners you can pay in installments through the bank installment programs of PrivatBank and monobank, without an overpayment for up to 6 months.
