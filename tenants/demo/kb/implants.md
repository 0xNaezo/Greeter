# Dental implants

Implant treatment starts with a consultation with a CT-based plan. The implant (a titanium root) is placed under local anaesthesia in one visit. After a healing period of about 3-6 months, a crown is made on the implant.

We use Swiss and Korean implant systems with a manufacturer's lifetime warranty on the implant. The price in the price list is for the implant without the crown; the full cost is given in your treatment plan. Installment payment is available.
