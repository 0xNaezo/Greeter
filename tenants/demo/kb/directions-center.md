# Lumina Center: how to get there

Address: 14 Volodymyrska St, Kyiv, ground floor with a separate entrance from the street.

Metro: Zoloti Vorota, about 5 minutes on foot. Parking: paid street parking along Volodymyrska St and an underground car park in the business centre next door. The entrance is step-free, and there is a wheelchair-accessible restroom.
