# In-office whitening

In-office whitening lightens teeth by several shades in one visit of about 90 minutes. Before whitening you need a professional cleaning (within the last 6 months) and no untreated cavities; the doctor checks this first.

Whitening does not change the colour of fillings, crowns or veneers. Temporary sensitivity for 1-2 days is common. For 48 hours after whitening follow a "white diet": no coffee, tea, red wine, berries, sauces or smoking.

Whitening is not done during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for patients under 18.
