# Children's dentistry

Our paediatric dentist sees children aged 3-14. The first visit is a gentle check-up and an introduction, so the child gets used to the clinic. A parent or legal guardian must accompany patients under 18.

We recommend a check-up and fluoride varnish every 6 months. Children's fillings are done with local anaesthesia; we do not use general anaesthesia or sedation.
