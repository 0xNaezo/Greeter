# After a tooth extraction

Keep the gauze pad in place for 20-30 minutes. For the first 24 hours do not rinse your mouth, do not drink through a straw, avoid hot food and drinks, alcohol, smoking and sport. Eat soft food on the other side.

A cold compress on the cheek (15 minutes on, 15 minutes off) helps against swelling in the first hours. Mild pain and swelling for 2-3 days are normal.

Contact us, or the emergency line, if bleeding does not stop, swelling grows after the third day, or you have a fever.
