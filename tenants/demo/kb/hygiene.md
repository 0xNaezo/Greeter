# Professional cleaning

Professional hygiene removes tartar and plaque: ultrasonic scaling, Air-Flow polishing of stains, final polishing and a fluoride varnish. It takes about an hour and is done by our hygienist.

We recommend it every 6 months. Teeth may be a little sensitive for a day or two. For 2 hours after the cleaning avoid coloured food and drinks (coffee, tea, red wine, beetroot) and smoking.
