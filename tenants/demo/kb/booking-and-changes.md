# Booking, rescheduling and cancelling

You can book a visit in this chat, by phone at either branch, or at the reception desk. The assistant shows free times and asks you to confirm the details with a button before anything is booked.

We send reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit, with buttons to confirm, reschedule or cancel.

Please reschedule or cancel at least 12 hours in advance so another patient can take the time. There is no cancellation fee. If you cannot come at the last moment, just let us know in the chat.
