# Wisdom teeth

Wisdom teeth are removed by our oral surgeon. Before the operation you need a panoramic X-ray, sometimes a CT scan, to see the position of the roots. The operation is done under local anaesthesia and usually takes 30-60 minutes.

Swelling and discomfort for 2-4 days are normal; the surgeon gives written aftercare instructions and can issue a sick-leave note. Stitches, if any, are removed after 7-10 days.
